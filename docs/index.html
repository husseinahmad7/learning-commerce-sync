01 / System design

Five decisions that keep the systems separate

01

Keep learning in the LMS

Next.js owns public pages and course discovery. Where an LMS catalog is connected, use a supported API. Open edX retains its course player and authoring tools.

02

Make the handoff obvious

Give users a direct course link and an easy way back. Match navigation and branding without forking the LMS. A reverse proxy can route URLs; it does not create a login session.

03

Share identity, not cookies

The storefront and LMS are separate clients of one identity provider. Each completes its own sign-in and owns its own session. The provider’s existing session can spare the learner another password prompt.

04

Record payment before delivery

Store confirmed payment and its outbox entry in the same database transaction. Workers deliver the work; stable identifiers let retries avoid duplicating invoices and enrollments.

05

Preserve uncertainty

Unsent, rejected, and unknown invoices need different responses. Keep the tracking record outside the accounting service, so operators can see unfinished work while that service is down.
